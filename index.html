03 — PROCESS

How It's Made

Every dataset is generated synthetically — no real user data, no scraped personal information, no third-party records. This means datasets can be produced, scaled, and customised without the privacy or licensing concerns that come with real-world data sources.

Each dataset goes through a structured generation and validation process to check for consistency, schema accuracy, and realistic data distributions before delivery.
