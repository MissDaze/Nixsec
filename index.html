Buyer questions

Clear before commitment.

Can I assess the data before purchasing?
Yes. Download the free evaluation samples and case studies. If the structure is close but not exact, send your required fields and we can quote a tailored version.
Can you change the schema or record volume?
Yes. Fields, volumes, facility mix, time period and deliberately modelled operational patterns can be adjusted to suit a defined use case.
Does the data contain personal or health information?
No. These products are born synthetic and contain no records from real patients, workers or facilities.
What can the datasets be used for?
Analytics, dashboards, research, education, model development, evaluation, demonstrations and software testing. They are not clinically validated and must not guide real patient care.
How do I purchase?
Email your chosen dataset and intended use. We confirm the package, licence, price and delivery details before payment. You can also contact NixSec through the DataRade storefront.

Need data that fits your system?

Send the schema, volume and outcome you want to test. We aim to reply within one business day.
