Buyer questions

Clear before commitment.

Can I assess the data before purchasing?
Yes. Download the free evaluation samples and case studies. If the structure is close but not exact, send your required fields and we can quote a tailored version.
Can you change the schema or record volume?
Yes. For custom work, we can discuss fields, volumes, time period and modelled patterns. Scope depends on the relevant collection and data source.
Does the data contain personal or health information?
Our three synthetic healthcare datasets contain no records of real patients, staff or facilities. Our separate professional-profile B2B products do include sourced work-related contact details; their licensing and permitted use are assessed separately.
What can the datasets be used for?
Permitted uses depend on the dataset and agreed licence. Synthetic healthcare data suits testing, demonstrations and education, never real patient care. B2B research is subject to privacy, source and direct-marketing rules. Research benchmarks do not establish investment performance.
How do I purchase?
Email your chosen dataset and intended use. We confirm the package, licence, price and delivery details before payment. You can also contact NixSec through the DataRade storefront.

Need data that fits your system?

Tell us the data category, fields, coverage, intended use and preferred licence. We will confirm the available scope and provide a quotation.
