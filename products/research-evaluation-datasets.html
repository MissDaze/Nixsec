Retail Product Breakout Research

Historical evaluation covering 2,825 product SKUs, strict future-growth labels, negative examples, alternative feature sets and benchmark results. This is a useful failed-model evaluation case: strict early-warning models achieved 0 qualifying hits among their top 60 alerts. It is not offered as a profitable product-prediction signal.

Underlying public-source redistribution conditions, derived-work licensing and packaging are confirmed before buyer delivery.

Commodity Event-Reaction Research

700 distinct documented announcements and 1,752 event–instrument feature records with time alignment, reaction features and model evaluations. Useful for temporal ML evaluation, event extraction and backtesting-methodology testing. The tested research did not establish a repeatable trading advantage. Raw benchmark market-price redistribution rights are checked per licence.

Synthetic Forensic Document Evaluation

One fictional forensic-nursing case benchmark with 25 evidence documents, assessment instructions and a separate scoring key. Intended for document comprehension, chronology, contradiction identification and controlled reasoning evaluation. Fictional benchmark content must not be presented as real case evidence.
