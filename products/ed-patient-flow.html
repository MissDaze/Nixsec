Designed analytical signals

The generator models interpretable relationships between arrival mode, urgency, occupancy, waiting time, length of stay and disposition. Higher occupancy produces longer modelled waits and stays. Ambulance and police arrivals skew toward more urgent ATS categories, while bed block is concentrated in admission pathways.

Responsible-use statement

Every patient, clinician and facility is fictional. The dataset contains no personal or health information from real people and is not clinically validated. It is designed for research, education, analytics, AI/ML development and software testing—not patient care or real-facility benchmarking.
