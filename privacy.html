02 — INFORMATION

Information we may collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you interact with NixSec.

This may include:

  • Your name

When you visit the NixSec website, standard technical information may be processed automatically by our hosting infrastructure or other service providers.

04 — HOW WE USE IT

How personal information is used

We may use personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries
  • Provide quotes for custom data products
  • Process or fulfil purchases
  • Deliver datasets, files and supporting resources
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain business and transaction records
  • Improve our products and website
  • Detect misuse, fraud or security issues
  • Meet legal, tax or regulatory obligations

We do not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Payment providers

Where purchases are processed through a third-party payment provider, such as PayPal or another payment platform, payment details may be processed directly by that provider.

NixSec does not need to receive or store your full credit card or banking details where payment is handled entirely through a third-party payment processor.

NixSec may use third-party services to operate the business and website.

These may include:

  • Website hosting providers
  • Cloud infrastructure providers
  • Code and file hosting platforms
  • Email providers
  • Payment processors
  • Marketplace platforms
  • Analytics or security tools

Personal information is only shared where reasonably necessary to provide the relevant service, process a transaction, comply with legal obligations or protect the security of NixSec and its users.

07 — DATA PRODUCTS

Synthetic data and personal information

NixSec produces synthetic data products designed to contain generated records rather than real personal records.

Our synthetic healthcare datasets do not intentionally contain real patient identities, real patient records, scraped personal information or identifiable clinical records.

Synthetic data is created for purposes such as analytics, research, education, AI development, testing and prototyping.

Synthetic data products should not be interpreted as records of real people, clinicians, facilities or events unless explicitly stated otherwise.
08 — STORAGE

Data retention and security

NixSec takes reasonable steps to protect personal information from unauthorised access, misuse, loss, disclosure or alteration.

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably required for business, contractual, support, legal, tax or regulatory purposes.

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. However, we aim to minimise the amount of personal information collected and use appropriate services and practices to protect information under our control.
